How early or late the buses are

Each bus arrival compared with the nearest timetabled bus at that stop.

Times at the first stop come straight from the timetable. At later stops they add the estimated ride time, so part of any difference there is the estimate, not the bus. Arrivals more than 20 minutes from any timetabled bus are left out.
Reports of the same route at the same stop within 2 minutes count as one arrival, so a bus five people reported counts once. One person reporting it twice counts as two buses. Two buses under 2 minutes apart, each reported by someone different, count as one.
